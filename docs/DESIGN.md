# Design

Gym floor first: tired, sweaty, one hand, phone at arm's length on a bench, between sets.

- **Gym-first ergonomics.** Huge tap targets, one-thumb reach, fat type readable at arm's length, zero precision gestures mid-set.
- **Dark and light, both first-class.** Neither is an afterthought.
- **A single lime accent.** One accent, nothing else. Not green (reads as *success*), not amber (reads as *warning*) — the commit button means "this logs the set" and nothing more.
- **Calm and dense.** The workout screen shows what the next set needs. The pleasure is speed and clarity, not decoration.
- **No surprises.** Every tap goes where it obviously goes.

Bar: Aplorio's level of intention — worked tokens, consistency, cared-for details — not its visual identity.

Not this: decoration that slows the loop, feature-dense screens that require learning, desktop-shaped forms squeezed onto a phone.

Type and feel are judged on the phone at arm's length, never from a specimen sheet.

Tokens, the type scale and the touch-target sizes live in `src/app.css`, with their reasoning.
